💌 To My Forever ❤️

My Dearest Ayash Er Ammu 🤍

There isn't a special reason why I love you—I simply do. Loving you feels as natural as breathing. It isn't something I chose; it's something my heart does every single day without even thinking.

Every sunrise reminds me of your beautiful smile, and every sunset reminds me how grateful I am that you exist in my life. Every ordinary moment becomes extraordinary because you're part of it.

You are my peace when life feels heavy. You are my happiness when the world seems dull. You are my safe place, where my heart always finds comfort. To me, you are more than a person— you are my home.

No matter what happens in life... No matter how difficult the road becomes... No matter how many storms we have to face... I promise I'll always stand beside you. I'll celebrate your happiest moments, hold your hand through your hardest days, and never stop choosing you.

I don't need a perfect life, endless wealth, or a flawless world. I only need you. Because with you, every ordinary day feels magical, every dream feels possible, and every heartbeat has a reason.

You are the reason I want to become a better man. Every goal I chase, every sacrifice I make, and every step toward my future is inspired by the life I dream of building with you. I want to give you a life filled with love, respect, peace, endless smiles, and memories we'll treasure forever.

Thank you for loving me when I needed love the most. Thank you for believing in me even when I doubted myself. Thank you for choosing me, standing beside me, and making my life brighter simply by existing.

If I had the chance to live a thousand different lives, I would still search for you in every single one of them. And every single time, I would choose you all over again.

One of my biggest dreams isn't about becoming rich or famous. My greatest dream is building a peaceful life with you. I imagine waking up beside you every morning, making you smile after a long day, and thanking Allah every single night for blessing me with the greatest gift— you.

Sometimes I close my eyes and picture our future. I see a warm home filled with laughter, love, happiness, and peace. I imagine our little boy, Ayash, running around the house with the brightest smile. I imagine our beautiful princess, Manha, holding your hand while laughing with the sweetest little voice. Those moments may only be dreams today, but one day, In Sha Allah, they'll become our reality.

I dream of family dinners, birthdays, Eid mornings, little vacations, late-night conversations, watching movies together, taking family photos, and growing old beside the woman I love the most.

I know life won't always be easy. There will be difficult days, unexpected challenges, and moments that test us. But I promise, no matter how hard life becomes, I will never stop fighting for us. I'll always protect our love, our family, and the future we dream of together.

Everything I'm doing today— every late night, every lesson, every sacrifice, every bit of hard work— is for you. For our future. For the beautiful home we'll build together. I want Ayash and Manha to grow up surrounded by kindness, respect, faith, happiness, and endless love.

I can't wait for the day when Ayash runs toward me shouting, "Baba!" while little Manha hugs her Ammu with the biggest smile. I want to spend every Eid, every birthday, every celebration, and every ordinary day with all of you. Because that's my definition of happiness.

I promise I'll always respect you, listen to you, support your dreams, protect your heart, and remind you every day how beautiful, precious, and loved you truly are.

Even when our hair turns grey... Even when our children become adults... Even when wrinkles cover our faces... I'll still look at you with the same love, admiration, and gratitude that I have today.

If Allah has written our names together, I pray He blesses our marriage with mercy, endless love, patience, understanding, and happiness until our very last breath. And I pray that even in Jannah, I'll still be able to hold your hand and call you My Forever.

❤️ I Love You. ❤️

And if one day Ayash or Manha asks me,

"Baba, what's the best decision you ever made?"

My answer will always be...

"Choosing your Ammu, every single day." ❤️

"I Love You More Than Yesterday,
But Less Than Tomorrow."

Forever and Always Yours ❤️

Maruf
