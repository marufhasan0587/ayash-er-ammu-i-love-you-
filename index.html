# My Dearest Ayash Er Ammu 🤍 There isn't a special reason why I love you—I simply do. Loving you feels as natural as breathing. It isn't something I chose; it's something my heart does every single day without even thinking. Every sunrise reminds me of your beautiful smile, and every sunset reminds me how grateful I am that you exist in my life. Every ordinary moment becomes extraordinary because you're part of it. You are my peace when life feels heavy. You are my happiness when the world seems dull. You are my safe place, where my heart always finds comfort. To me, you are more than a person—you are my home. No matter what happens in life... no matter how difficult the road becomes... no matter how many storms we have to face... I promise I will always stand beside you. I'll celebrate your happiest moments, hold your hand through your hardest days, and never stop choosing you. I don't need a perfect life, endless wealth, or a flawless world. I only need you. Because with you, every ordinary day feels magical, every dream feels possible, and every heartbeat has a reason. You are the reason I want to become a better man. Every goal I chase, every sacrifice I make, and every step I take toward my future is inspired by the life I dream of building with you. I want to give you a life filled with love, respect, peace, and endless smiles. Thank you for loving me when I needed love the most. Thank you for believing in me, even when I doubted myself. Thank you for choosing me, standing by my side, and making my life brighter simply by being in it. If I had the chance to live a thousand different lives, I would still search for you in every single one of them. And every time, I would choose you all over again. **I love you more than yesterday, but less than tomorrow.** Every new day, my love for you grows stronger than the day before. **Forever and Always Yours,** ❤️ **Maruf**
