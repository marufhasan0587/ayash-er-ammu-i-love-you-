💌 To My Forever ❤️

My Dearest Ayash Er Ammu 🤍

There isn't a special reason why I love you—I simply do. Loving you feels as natural as breathing. It isn't something I chose; it's something my heart does every single day without even thinking.

Every sunrise reminds me of your beautiful smile, and every sunset reminds me how grateful I am that you exist in my life. Every ordinary moment becomes extraordinary because you're part of it.

You are my peace when life feels heavy. You are my happiness when the world seems dull. You are my safe place, where my heart always finds comfort. To me, you are more than a person—you are my home.

No matter what happens in life... No matter how difficult the road becomes... No matter how many storms we have to face... I promise I'll always stand beside you. I'll celebrate your happiest moments, hold your hand through your hardest days, and never stop choosing you.

I don't need a perfect life, endless wealth, or a flawless world. I only need you. Because with you, every ordinary day feels magical, every dream feels possible, and every heartbeat has a reason.

You are the reason I want to become a better man. Every goal I chase, every sacrifice I make, and every step toward my future is inspired by the life I dream of building with you. I want to give you a life filled with love, respect, peace, and endless smiles.

Thank you for loving me when I needed love the most. Thank you for believing in me even when I doubted myself. Thank you for choosing me, standing by my side, and making my life brighter simply by being in it.

If I had the chance to live a thousand different lives, I would still search for you in every single one of them. And every time, I would choose you all over again.

One of my biggest dreams isn't about becoming rich or famous. My greatest dream is building a peaceful life with you. I often imagine the little moments we'll share together—waking up beside you every morning, making you smile after a long day, and thanking Allah every night for blessing me with you.

Sometimes I close my eyes and picture our future. I see a warm home filled with laughter, love, and endless happiness. I imagine our little boy Ayash running around the house with the brightest smile, while our beautiful daughter Manha holds your hand and fills our hearts with joy. I dream of us sitting together at the dinner table, celebrating birthdays, making unforgettable memories, and growing old side by side.

I know life won't always be easy. There will be difficult days, challenges, and moments that test us. But I promise that no matter what happens, I will never stop fighting for us. I want to become the kind of husband you can always rely on and the kind of father our children can always be proud of.

Everything I'm doing today—every late night, every sacrifice, every lesson I learn—is for our future. Every step I take is for you, for our family, and for the beautiful life we dream of together. I want to build a home where your smile never fades, where Ayash and Manha grow up surrounded by love, respect, kindness, and happiness.

I can't wait for the day when Ayash runs to me shouting, "Baba!", and little Manha smiles while holding your hand. I dream of celebrating every birthday together, taking family photos, going on little trips, praying together, and creating memories we'll cherish forever.

When life becomes difficult, I don't want us to give up on each other. I want us to hold hands even tighter, remind each other why we started this journey, and prove that true love isn't about perfect days— it's about staying together through every season of life.

If Allah has written our names together, I promise to protect your heart, respect you, support your dreams, and love you with everything I have. Even when our hair turns grey and we grow old together, I'll still look at you with the same love I have today.

❤️ I Love You. ❤️

And if one day Ayash or Manha asks me,
"Baba, what's the best decision you ever made?"

My answer will always be:

"Choosing your Ammu, every single day." ❤️
